# MemeLab Production Authority Policy

## Final approved policy

### Rocket Rat — RKTROT

- Total supply: 1,000,000,000
- Mint authority: revoke after the initial 1,000,000,000 tokens are minted
- Freeze authority: none
- Metadata update authority: revoke after final metadata verification
- Future minting: disabled
- Freeze capability: disabled
- Metadata: immutable after final verification

### Degen Rat — DGRAT

- Total supply: 1,000,000,000
- Mint authority: revoke after the initial 1,000,000,000 tokens are minted
- Freeze authority: none
- Metadata update authority: revoke after final metadata verification
- Future minting: disabled
- Freeze capability: disabled
- Metadata: immutable after final verification

## Irreversible actions

The following actions are intentionally irreversible once executed:

1. Revoking mint authority.
2. Removing freeze authority.
3. Clearing metadata update authority.

No production mint transaction may be signed until the final launch gate confirms that the metadata and artwork have been verified.
